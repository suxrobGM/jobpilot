# The Pilot

The Pilot is JobPilot's autonomous mode. Instead of running a skill each time,
you write your **instructions** once - your goals and limits - and the local agent runs
your search on its own: perpetual cycles that discover and score roles, apply,
find warm intros before cold applies, send and follow up on outreach, review
recruiter replies, and draft interview replies, prep sheets, and self-promotion
posts. It journals every action, sends the few decisions only you can make
to your phone as questions, and keeps going overnight with the browser closed. You wake to a
morning digest.

The Pilot **supersedes** the [campaign modes](/docs/campaigns-and-skills) - it
runs all of them for you - but those skills stay available for hands-on work.

## Enabling it

1. Make sure your local agent terminal is running (the dock in the dashboard's
   bottom corner). If it's offline, install or start it first - see
   [Getting started](/docs/getting-started).
2. Open **Pilot** in the workspace.
3. Write your instructions (below) and save them.
4. Click **Enable**.

Enabling pairs your machine with the Pilot once, so you don't need a browser tab
open after that: the local host self-starts cycles on boot, crash, or wake and
drives the loop until you disable it.

## Your instructions

Your instructions are one small document that steers everything. Its fields:

- **Goals** - plain-language direction: the roles you want, priorities, and
  constraints (for example, "senior TypeScript remote, at least $150k, by
  October").
- **Operating limits** - daily apply cap, daily outreach cap, follow-up delay,
  minimum match score, and how often the Pilot checks for work.
- **Active hours** - restrict cycles to a window (with a time zone), or leave
  off to run around the clock.
- **Saved searches** - searches the Pilot re-runs on a schedule, so new
  postings get scored and applied to without you starting a campaign.
- **Outreach autonomy** - per channel. Email can be **draft** (compose only),
  **review** (ask before each send), or **auto** (send within your cap).
  LinkedIn is always review - the Pilot never sends an InMail on its own.
- **Platforms** - where the Pilot drafts self-promotion posts (a
  "who wants to be hired" thread, a subreddit, a LinkedIn post) and how often.
  Posts are always review-only.

Soft judgment lives in your goals text; the caps are also enforced on the
server, so the Pilot can never exceed them no matter how you word your instructions.
Edit your instructions any time - even from your phone - and the change takes effect
on the next cycle.

## What it does on its own vs. what asks you

| Runs on its own | Always asks you |
| --- | --- |
| Applies to matching roles within your daily cap | A 2FA / login code (a one-tap card, ~5-minute window) |
| Sends outreach emails you set to auto, within your cap | Salary and screening questions on an application |
| Follows up on outreach per your autonomy setting | Sending a LinkedIn InMail |
| Reviews recruiter replies in your inbox (proposes moves) | Posting any self-promotion publicly |
| Drafts everything - emails, follow-ups, interview replies, prep sheets, posts | Parking a board that keeps failing |

Anything in the right column surfaces as a question. Nothing sends, posts, or
changes an application's status without you.

## Questions and push notifications

When the Pilot hits a decision only you can make, it posts a **question** - a
one-decision card in the Pilot page - and parks the related work until you
answer. Answer, and the parked job or message resumes on the next cycle.

To answer without watching the dashboard, turn on **Notifications** in the Pilot
page for each device (including your phone). You'll get a web push when a
question opens, even with the tab closed - tap it and answer the card. A 2FA
code request self-expires after about five minutes; if you miss it, the Pilot
skips that job cleanly and moves on.

## The journal and morning digest

Every action the Pilot takes lands in the **journal** - a live feed you can
watch in real time ("Applied to Staff TypeScript Engineer at Acme - score 87",
"Reviewed 7 replies - 1 interview invite", "Parked Stripe - needs your salary
answer"). Once a day it rolls the last 24 hours into a **morning digest**:
applications submitted, replies reviewed, outreach sent, and questions waiting.

Use **Export** on the journal card to download the full history as a file for
your own records.

## Interview autonomy

When a recruiter's reply is an interview invite, the Pilot drafts a short
availability reply and posts it as an approval card - it never sends it for you.
It also builds an interview **prep sheet** (behavioral, technical, system
design, and company notes) and attaches it to the application, so it's ready
when you approve the reply.

## Troubleshooting

- **Host offline** - the Pilot can't run without your local agent. Start it from
  the dock; the Pilot resumes on its own once the host is back.
- **Pilot paused after repeated stalls** - if cycles keep timing out, the host
  backs off to protect your machine. Check the dock terminal for what wedged,
  then re-enable.
- **A board keeps failing** - the Pilot probes it once, and if it still fails,
  asks whether to park it. Parked boards are skipped until you clear them; the
  rest of your search keeps running.
