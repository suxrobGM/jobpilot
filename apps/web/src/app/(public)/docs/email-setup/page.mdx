# Email setup

When you connect Gmail, JobPilot can do three more things:

- Match recruiter replies to your applications.
- Read the sign-in codes that job boards email you while it's applying.
- Send networking emails from your own address.

You connect Gmail through a Google OAuth client that you create yourself.
JobPilot doesn't have a shared Google app, so your mail never goes through
anyone else's, and Google doesn't need to review anything. You only do this
once, and it takes about five minutes.

## Create your OAuth client

1. In the [Google Cloud Console](https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/credentials),
   create a project. Then create an OAuth 2.0 Client ID and choose
   **Web application** as the type.
2. Under "APIs & Services", turn on the **Gmail API** for the project.
3. In JobPilot, open **Settings → Email** and copy the **redirect URI**. Add
   it to your OAuth client as an authorized redirect URI.
4. Go to
   [Google Auth Platform → Data access](https://console.cloud.google.com/auth/scopes)
   and press **Add or remove scopes**. Search for **`gmail.readonly`** and
   **`gmail.send`**, tick both Gmail API rows, and press **Save**. The first
   lets JobPilot read replies and sign-in codes. The second lets it send
   networking emails.
5. Leave the app in Testing mode and add your Gmail address under
   [Audience → Test users](https://console.cloud.google.com/auth/audience).
   You're the only user of your own project, so Google never needs to verify
   it. The downside is that Google signs you out of Testing-mode apps after
   about seven days, so expect to reconnect about once a week. Publishing the
   app doesn't help. Gmail access is restricted, and a published app that
   Google hasn't verified can't sign in at all.
6. Back in **Settings → Email**, paste the **Client ID** and **Client
   secret**, save, and press **Connect Gmail**.

## What JobPilot can access

JobPilot can read your mail and send mail. It can't delete or change
anything. Google's consent screen lists four permissions: `gmail.readonly` and
`gmail.send` do the work, and `openid` and `email` only tell JobPilot which
address you connected.

Your client ID and secret are encrypted and stored with your account. The
secret stays on the server and is never sent to your browser. The connected
Gmail account is linked to your profile and nobody else's.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Access blocked: app has not completed the Google verification
  process".** Your Gmail address isn't on the test users list. Add it under
  **Audience → Test users**.
- **403 "Request had insufficient authentication scopes".** One of the Gmail
  permissions is missing. Add both under **Data access**, then disconnect and
  connect again in **Settings → Email** so Google issues a new token.
- **Gmail connects, but networking can't send.** You connected before adding
  `gmail.send`. Add it under **Data access**, then press
  **Reconnect to enable sending** in **Settings → Email**.
- **Google shows a 500 error after you published the app.** Published apps
  that use Gmail need Google's review. Switch back under
  **Audience → Publishing status → Back to testing**.

Gmail is the only email provider for now. Outlook and IMAP are planned.
