# Email setup

With Gmail connected, JobPilot can:

- match recruiter replies to your applications,
- read sign-in codes that job boards email you,
- send networking emails from your address.

You connect through a Google OAuth client you create yourself. There's no
shared JobPilot app, so your mail never passes through anyone else's. It takes
about five minutes, once.

## Create your OAuth client

1. In the [Google Cloud Console](https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/credentials),
   create a project, then an **OAuth 2.0 Client ID** of type
   **Web application**.
2. Under **APIs & Services**, turn on the **Gmail API**.
3. In JobPilot, open **Settings → Email** and copy the **redirect URI**. Add
   it to your OAuth client as an authorized redirect URI.
4. Open [Google Auth Platform → Data access](https://console.cloud.google.com/auth/scopes),
   press **Add or remove scopes**, tick **`gmail.readonly`** and
   **`gmail.send`**, and press **Save**.
5. Leave the app in **Testing** and add your Gmail address under
   [Audience → Test users](https://console.cloud.google.com/auth/audience).
6. Back in **Settings → Email**, paste the **Client ID** and **Client
   secret**, save, and press **Connect Gmail**.

Google signs you out of Testing apps after about seven days, so expect to
reconnect once a week. JobPilot shows a notice when it's time. Publishing the
app doesn't help: Gmail access is restricted, and an unverified published app
can't sign in at all.

## What JobPilot can access

It can read and send mail. It can't delete or change anything. Google's
consent screen lists four permissions: `gmail.readonly` and `gmail.send` do the
work; `openid` and `email` only identify the connected address.

Your client ID and secret are encrypted with your account's key. The secret
stays on the server and never reaches your browser.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Access blocked: app has not completed the Google verification
  process".** Your address isn't a test user. Add it under
  **Audience → Test users**.
- **403 "Request had insufficient authentication scopes".** A Gmail scope is
  missing. Add both under **Data access**, then disconnect and connect again
  in **Settings → Email**.
- **Gmail connects, but networking can't send.** You connected before adding
  `gmail.send`. Add it, then press **Reconnect to enable sending** in
  **Settings → Email**.
- **Google shows a 500 error after you published the app.** Switch back under
  **Audience → Publishing status → Back to testing**.

## Microsoft 365 and shared mailboxes

Select **Microsoft 365** in **Settings → Email**. This integration supports work/school accounts in the public Microsoft cloud, including a shared mailbox your Microsoft user can already access. Personal Outlook.com accounts, on-premises Exchange and IMAP are not supported by this provider.

1. In [Microsoft Entra](https://entra.microsoft.com/), create an app registration in your organization's tenant. Record its **Directory (tenant) ID** and **Application (client) ID**.
2. Add a **Web** platform redirect URI, copied exactly from JobPilot's Microsoft email configuration. For self-hosting, set `MICROSOFT_OAUTH_REDIRECT_URI` to the externally reachable API callback `/api/email/oauth/callback` and register that exact URL; the local default is `http://localhost:4101/api/email/oauth/callback`.
3. Add delegated Microsoft Graph permissions: `User.Read` and `Mail.Read` for your own mailbox, or `Mail.Read.Shared` for a shared mailbox. OAuth also requests `openid`, `email` and `offline_access`. Your organization's consent policy may require an administrator.
4. Create a client secret and enter its **value** (not its ID) in JobPilot, together with the tenant and client IDs. Secrets stay encrypted on JobPilot's server and are never returned to the browser. Do not put them in a URL or paste them into an agent conversation.
5. For your own work mailbox, leave the shared-mailbox address blank. For a shared mailbox, enter its address. Sign in as your own Microsoft user who has delegated access; do not sign in as the shared mailbox. JobPilot verifies access to the selected Inbox and will not substitute your personal Inbox if permission is denied.
6. Save, then **Connect Microsoft 365** and complete Microsoft's consent screen. Settings shows the mailbox being synced and the identity used to sign in.

Microsoft connections are **read-only by default**. Sync reads the Inbox, imports recent messages (14 days), follows incremental changes and feeds the same reply-matching, job-alert and review workflow as Gmail. It does not mark messages read, move them or delete them. Local import retention and review rules still apply.

To enable sending, opt into **Request permission to send mail** and reconnect. This requests `Mail.Send` for your own mailbox or `Mail.Send.Shared` for a shared mailbox. Shared-mailbox sending also requires Exchange **Send As** or **Send on Behalf** permission. Sending permission does not turn on Pilot networking; its controls remain separate. Microsoft accepts sends asynchronously and does not return a message ID from sendMail; incoming replies can still be matched through the existing sender/application matching.

One mailbox can be connected per JobPilot profile. Gmail and Microsoft app configurations are retained separately, but changing provider or mailbox requires disconnecting first. Disconnecting removes the local mailbox and its imported messages under the existing disconnect behavior; it does not delete mail from Microsoft. Reconnect after a token is revoked or expires beyond refresh; access denial requires fixing the delegate's permissions. Microsoft throttling is respected with bounded retries; interrupted sync never advances the saved cursor.

For access troubleshooting, see [Microsoft's shared-mailbox guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/outlook-share-messages-folders) and [sending from another mailbox](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/outlook-send-mail-from-other-user).
