# FAQ

## Do I need an API key?

No. The agent runs on your Claude Code or Codex subscription. JobPilot is free
and has no AI bill of its own.

## Which AI model should I use?

A mid-range one, like **Claude Sonnet 5.5** or **GPT 6 Luna**. JobPilot starts
on these by default.

Top models like **Claude Opus 5.5** don't send more applications. The work is
mostly reading postings and filling in forms, and a top model spends longer
thinking at each step. With the Pilot running all day, it can use a week's
limit in a day or two. To change the model anyway, type `/model` in the
terminal.

## Where does the agent run?

On your computer. The website stores your data, but the agent and the browser
it applies with run on your machine.

## How do I start the agent after it stops?

Open the agent drawer. On Windows, press **Start agent**; the button appears
after the agent's first run. On macOS and Linux, run setup again in Claude
Code or Codex, or type `jobpilot` in a terminal.

## Which job boards does it work with?

Any site you can open in a browser. New accounts start with LinkedIn, Indeed,
Hiring Cafe, and We Work Remotely. Wellfound, Y Combinator (Work at a
Startup), Welcome to the Jungle, Hacker News "Who's Hiring", Remote OK, 4 Day
Week, and Upwork are one click away. Add any other site on the **Job boards**
page.

## How do I tell the Pilot what jobs I want?

Say it in the Pilot page's chat, the way you'd brief a recruiter. Your first
message sets up searches, each carrying the reason behind it. Ask it any time
to add, pause, or change a search, or to run a focused web search.

**Pilot → Searches & rules** shows the searches, your deal-breakers, and the
lowest match score worth applying to. Every change, yours or the Pilot's, can
be undone for 7 days.

## What is autopilot?

With autopilot on, the Pilot picks its own work: new searches, applications,
replies, and networking. With it off, it runs only what you ask for in chat or
with a button. Queued work can be cancelled from the thread.

## How many jobs does it apply to each day?

Up to your daily limit, which the server enforces, so it holds even if
something goes wrong mid-task. Change it under **Pilot → Searches & rules**,
or ask in chat. The networking limit, under **Settings → Behavior**, works the
same way.

## What does the Pilot do first?

Your chat messages, then work you asked for, then questions you answered,
interview replies, applications (best matches first), networking, and new
searches. See [The Pilot](/docs/pilot).

## Will it send or post anything without asking?

Only what you've allowed: applications up to your limit, and networking emails
if email is set to **Auto-send**. LinkedIn messages, posts, and interview
replies always wait for your approval. The server enforces this.

## What if my computer sleeps or crashes mid-task?

Nothing is lost. Results are saved as the agent goes, and an unfinished task
goes back on the list. After a restart, start the agent again and it carries
on.

## Can I use JobPilot while the Pilot is running?

Yes. Buttons ask the Pilot for work, so it runs next. If you type in the
terminal, the Pilot waits 3 minutes after your last keystroke before starting
anything new. **Hand back** on the Pilot page ends the wait early.

## Can I see exactly what it did?

Yes. The thread lists every task and its result. The agent drawer's **Steps**
tab shows the running task step by step, on any device. **Settings →
Diagnostics** has every run, its token cost, and an export.

## How long do I have to answer a question?

As long as you like. The job waits. Login codes are the exception: they expire
in about five minutes, and if you miss one the Pilot skips that job. You're
only asked for codes from SMS or an authenticator app; emailed codes are read
from your inbox. Turn on **Settings → Notifications** to get questions on
your phone.

## Can JobPilot read and send email?

Yes, through a Google OAuth client you create. See
[Email setup](/docs/email-setup). It reads mail to match replies and pick up
sign-in codes, and sends networking emails. It can't delete mail. JobPilot
works without email; you just lose those features.

## What about captchas?

It solves checkbox and text captchas itself. Picture puzzles need a 2Captcha
or CapSolver key, added on the **Job boards** page (see
[Credentials](/docs/credentials)). Without one,
it skips the job and tells you why.

## How do I update the agent?

It checks for updates every time it starts. When a new version is out while
it's running, the agent drawer shows a banner. **Update now** installs it and
restarts the agent, ending the current session. **Prefer to update manually?**
shows the commands instead.

## Can I switch between Claude Code and Codex?

Yes, any time, from the agent drawer. Both use the same skills.

## Where are my resumes stored?

In your JobPilot account: uploaded PDFs and every tailored copy. Edit them
under **Resumes**.

## Does the agent change my resume?

Not without your approval.

- After you upload a resume, `review-resume` suggests a rewrite. **Review**
  shows each change; then **Apply rewrite** or **Discard**. Your PDF is kept,
  so **Re-extract from PDF** brings back the original.
- For each job, `tailor-resume` works on a separate copy. It may reorder,
  combine, or drop entries for a better fit, but never touches your main
  resume.

Neither can invent an employer, a date, or a number. The server rejects a
reworded bullet with a number the original didn't have, and works out the
dates of combined roles from the real ones. Anything it can't confirm is
flagged for you.

## Why do I keep getting rejected without interviews?

Look at how fast rejections arrive. Within a day usually means an automatic
filter, not a person, turned you down, often over work authorization,
location, or years of experience. What helps more than another resume edit:

- **Check the sponsorship question.** If you need sponsorship, many large
  companies filter on it first. The agent skips postings that say so, but
  most don't.
- **Apply to fewer, better-fitting companies.**
- **Use networking.** A referral skips the filter.
- **Connect your email** so rejections show up and you can see your response
  rate.

## How do I delete my data?

There's no delete button yet. Email
[suxrobgm@gmail.com](mailto:suxrobgm@gmail.com) from your sign-up address and
ask. Deleting an account removes your data and destroys your encryption key,
so stored secrets can never be read again.
